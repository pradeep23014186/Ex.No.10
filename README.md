<h2 align=center>SCENARIO 1: ARTICLE ON CLIMATE CHANGE</h2> 

### 1. FIRST DRAFT (Simple Prompt Used)

**Prompt Used:**
```
Write a simple article about climate change.
```

### First Draft Output

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns across the world. It is mainly caused by human activities such as burning fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial emissions. These actions release greenhouse gases like carbon dioxide into the atmosphere, trapping heat.

The effects of climate change include rising temperatures, melting ice caps, stronger storms, droughts, and unpredictable weather conditions. Many species are losing their habitats, and human societies face challenges like food shortages, water scarcity, and rising sea levels.

To reduce climate change, people must adopt renewable energy sources, protect forests, reduce waste, and support environmental policies. Climate change is a global issue that requires urgent and collective action.

### 2. REFINED VERSION (Using Prompt Techniques)

**Techniques Used:**

- Query Decomposition

- Decision Making

- Semantic Filtering

- Answer Engineering

- Fact-Check List

- Tone Control

**Prompt Used:**
```
Write a structured, fact-checked article on climate change. Include:
- Introduction
- Causes
- Effects
- Global Responses
- What individuals can do
  Use a formal yet easy-to-read tone. Ensure factual accuracy and avoid exaggeration.
```

### REFINED OUTPUT
**Introduction:**

Climate change represents the long-term alteration of global climate patterns, primarily driven by human activities since the Industrial Revolution. According to international climate organizations, global temperatures have increased by approximately 1.1°C since the late 19th century.

**Causes of Climate Change**

The primary cause is the increased concentration of greenhouse gases, especially carbon dioxide, methane, and nitrous oxide. These gases originate from:

- Burning fossil fuels

- Deforestation

- Industrial processes

- Agricultural activities

**Major Impacts**

Climate change has widespread consequences:

- Rising global temperatures

- Melting polar ice, contributing to sea-level rise

- Extreme weather events such as hurricanes, heatwaves, and droughts

- Loss of biodiversity, especially in coral reefs, mountain ecosystems, and coastal regions

- Human challenges, including food insecurity and displacement

**Global Responses**

Countries are adopting strategies to reduce emissions:

- Paris Agreement commitments

- Investments in renewable energy

- Reforestation programs

- Climate-resilient city planning

**Individual Actions**

Individuals can contribute by:

- Using public transportation

- Reducing energy use

- Supporting sustainable products

- Planting trees

- Advocating for climate-friendly policies

Climate change is not only an environmental issue but also a socioeconomic and ethical one, requiring both systemic and individual efforts.

### 3. MULTIPLE VERSIONS (Show Effect of Different Prompts)
#### Version A — Emotional Tone (Semantic Filter)

Climate change is the greatest threat to our planet’s future. Every year, the Earth grows warmer, storms grow stronger, and ecosystems collapse. Communities around the world are already suffering…

(More emotional, urgent tone.)

#### Version B — Highly Technical (Answer Engineering)

Climate change is driven by anthropogenic radiative forcing, primarily due to increased atmospheric CO₂ concentrations rising from 280 ppm pre-industrial to over 420 ppm today…

(More scientific, data-heavy.)

#### Version C — Story-Based (Tail Generation)

In a small coastal town, people once lived peacefully by the sea—until the tides began rising year after year…

(Narrative-driven explanation.)

### 4. FINAL VERSION (Polished Full Article)
**Climate Change: Understanding the Challenge and Shaping a Sustainable Future**

Climate change is one of the most pressing global challenges of the 21st century. Marked by rising temperatures, shifting climate patterns, and increasingly severe natural disasters, it affects ecosystems, economies, and societies worldwide.

Human activities—especially fossil fuel consumption, deforestation, and industrial processes—have increased greenhouse gas concentrations beyond natural levels. This intensifies the greenhouse effect, causing global warming.

The impacts are vast and interlinked: melting ice sheets raising sea levels, extreme heatwaves threatening agriculture, and disrupted rainfall patterns affecting freshwater supplies. Vulnerable populations face higher risks of displacement and poverty.

Governments and organizations worldwide are responding through agreements like the Paris Accord, renewable energy investments, and climate adaptation strategies. However, systemic action must be supported by individual choices—reducing energy use, recycling, sustainable consumption, and advocacy.

Climate change demands collective responsibility. Through informed decisions and coordinated global efforts, a sustainable and resilient future remains possible.

<h2 align=center>SCENARIO 2: RESEARCH PAPER ON ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE </h2> 

### 1. FIRST DRAFT (Simple Prompt Used)

**Prompt Used:**
```
Write a basic research-style explanation of artificial intelligence.
```

### First Draft Output

Artificial intelligence (AI) refers to machines or software that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence. AI includes areas like machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision. It helps automate repetitive tasks, analyze data, and make predictions.

Many industries use AI such as healthcare, business, manufacturing, and transportation. AI continues to improve and is expected to shape the future of technology.

### 2. REFINED VERSION (Using Advanced Prompt Techniques)

**Techniques Used:**

- Query Decomposition

- Answer Engineering

- Fact Checking

- Decision Making

- Semantic Filtering

- Structured Format

**Prompt Used:**
```
Write a proper research paper-style content on Artificial Intelligence.
Include:
- Abstract
- Introduction
- Literature Review
- Methodologies (Machine Learning, Deep Learning)
- Applications
- Challenges & Ethical Issues
- Conclusion
  Use a formal academic tone with factual accuracy.”
```

### Refined Output
**Abstract**

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has evolved from rule-based systems to advanced deep learning architectures capable of autonomous decision-making. This paper examines key AI methodologies, reviews significant academic contributions, and evaluates AI’s applications and ethical challenges.

**Introduction**

Artificial Intelligence is the simulation of human cognitive functions by machines. Modern AI systems use algorithms that learn from data, enabling tasks such as pattern detection, prediction, and natural language understanding.

**Literature Review**

Early AI research (1950–1980) focused on symbolic reasoning. Later, breakthroughs in machine learning and neural networks (1986–present) shifted focus to data-driven intelligence. Recent studies highlight the success of deep learning in fields like vision and language.

**Methodologies**
1. Machine Learning

Machine Learning enables systems to learn patterns from data using algorithms like decision trees, support vector machines, and clustering models.

2. Deep Learning

Deep Learning models use multi-layer neural networks—especially CNNs, RNNs, and Transformers—to achieve state-of-the-art results in language, images, and autonomous control.

**Applications**

AI is applied in:

- Healthcare diagnostics

- Financial fraud detection

- Autonomous vehicles

- Robotics

- Natural language processing

**Challenges and Ethics**

Key issues include:

- Data privacy

- Algorithmic bias

- Transparency

- Job displacement

- Security threats

**Conclusion**

AI continues to transform modern society through automation and enhanced decision-making. Responsible development is essential to maintain ethical standards and minimize societal risks.

### 3. MULTIPLE VERSIONS
#### Version A — Simplified Explanation

AI helps computers think like humans. It learns from data and improves itself…

#### Version B — Highly Technical Research Tone

Artificial Intelligence systems operate through stochastic gradient descent optimization applied to high-dimensional parameter spaces…

#### Version C — Creative Analogy

AI is like a student that learns by reading millions of books…

### 4. FINAL VERSION (Full Polished Research Content)
**Artificial Intelligence: A Comprehensive Research Overview**

Artificial Intelligence (AI) encompasses computational systems capable of performing cognitive tasks such as reasoning, learning, perception, and decision-making. Over the past decade, AI has grown rapidly, driven by improvements in computational power, algorithm design, and large-scale data availability.

Modern AI methodologies include machine learning and deep learning. Machine learning focuses on general algorithms that learn patterns, while deep learning uses multi-layer neural networks to perform complex tasks with high accuracy.

AI’s applications span healthcare, finance, education, manufacturing, and cybersecurity. From early disease detection to fraud analysis and smart automation, AI continues to revolutionize industries.

However, challenges persist—data privacy, fairness, transparency, model bias, and job displacement. Ethical frameworks and regulation are critical to ensuring that AI remains beneficial to society.

AI represents both an opportunity and a responsibility. Understanding its foundations and impacts is essential to guide its development toward a trustworthy and sustainable future.
